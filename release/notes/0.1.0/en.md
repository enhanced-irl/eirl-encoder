First public release of the EnhancedIRL encoder software for the Jetson Nano.

- Multi-camera mixer with a touch screen: the board's HDMI input, USB capture cards and webcams, and phones or cameras over RTMP/SRT, on the encoder's own Wi-Fi or on your network. Sources 1 to 4 can be given a name.
- Streams over SRT or bonded SRTLA, with adaptive bitrate.
- Web page and touch screen in English, Spanish and French, with a setup wizard on first start.
- Automatic updates: the encoder tells you when a new version is available and installs it when you press "Update". If the new version does not start correctly, the previous one comes back by itself.
