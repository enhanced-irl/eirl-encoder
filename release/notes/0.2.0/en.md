- Every internet connection now shows its state with a coloured dot: green (fine), amber (weak signal or struggling), red (no signal, no internet or disconnected) and grey (switched off). You see it in the touch screen's header, in Settings › Stream › Connections and in the new Connections card of the web page. A connection through a phone or a USB modem that has no internet (a SIM without data, for example) turns red and stops being used for the stream until it recovers.
- Everything you can set on the web page can now also be set on the touch screen: destination, connections, input, network cameras, the encoder's Wi-Fi, the Pause, audio, the EnhancedIRL link and the web password. Passwords and keys show as dots, with an eye to reveal them.
- Fades between cameras are smooth, and on the touch screen the small views of the sources change at the same time as the program.
